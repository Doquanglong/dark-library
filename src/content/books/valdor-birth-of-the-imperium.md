# Synopsis

Set generations before the Great Crusade, in the years after the Unification of Terra. Constantin Valdor, first Captain-General of the Legio Custodes, is placed under formal enquiry by Uwoma Kandawire, a High Lord of Terra investigating what the Custodes did at Ararat — and what became of the Thunder Warriors who won the Emperor his world.

# Chapters

## 1 · Sevuu

Kandawire travels incognito to an archaeological dig at Ararat, site of a massacre, with Sevuu, a local military attache she trusts. The scanners pick up an exotic heat-resistant ceramic in the debris, and flakes of gold that fry the equipment outright — ceramite and auramite, unknown to ordinary people at this point. She shuts the dig down as soon as she has what she came for: confirmation that the Custodes were here.

Her guards wear heavy chainmail that protects against nothing. The book keeps returning to it.

## 2 · Samonas

Samonas, a Custodian intelligence specialist, crosses the half-built Imperial Palace to meet Valdor. He suggests they simply kill Kandawire, since she knows. Valdor refuses — it is a new age, and he will not give in to a warlord's instincts. Watch her instead, and wait for a slip.

Valdor on the Emperor: he does not think He trusts anyone. The stronger you become, the weaker everything around you is, until there is nothing you can hold without breaking it.

## 3 · Kandawire

Kandawire arrives at the Palace and is put in quarters with no heat and no clean water. On the way to the Tower of the Hegemon she remembers her childhood in the Banda Confederacy — a guarded family compound, no food security, her mother dead of a cancer the Imperium can now cure, her father dead in the raid she fled.

She meets Valdor, sits before she realises she has obeyed, and places him under formal enquiry under the Lex Pacifica. He agrees without argument and asks what she wants to know.

## 4 · Ophar / Kandawire

Ophar — the servant who dragged her out of Banda as a girl, now her man for the dangerous work — buys smuggled customs records from an underworld contact. Shipments are going into the innermost Palace construction site with clearance from somewhere near the top of the Senatorum, and not one crate has been skimmed, which his contact has never seen in his life.

Kandawire begins her recorded interview with Valdor. She opens with Maulland Sen.

## 5 · Audio transcript

Valdor recounts the conquest of Maulland Sen, 126 years earlier — a kingdom of witches at the roof of the world, which the Emperor had marked out early because he had been there before and knew what was practised there. Valdor describes ghosts dogging the advance and, asked whether he means that figuratively, says plainly that he does not.

It was the first full-Legio deployment of the Thunder Warriors, the Fourth under the primarch Ushotan. They broke the citadel and then slaughtered everyone inside it, including slaves who might have been spared. Valdor's verdict: they were like the munitions of the day, powerful and unstable, and impossible to recall once loosed. Afterwards Ushotan told him he finally understood why he had been made, and pitied Valdor for never being able to feel it.

Valdor ends the session on the question of where that feeling led him.

## 6 · Kandawire / Liora

Ophar reports that the Custodes have pulled off the patrol routes to concentrate on the Senatorum, and are going somewhere underground. There are far fewer of them on Terra than anyone assumed. The Palace walls have been handed to the Castellan Guard, a regiment Kandawire has never heard of. Meanwhile illegal small-arms are being waved through customs in quantity. Kandawire gives the signal.

Liora, a technician deep in the Dungeon, works on gene-craft under a dozen security stops — noting a flaw as "sanguinary obsessive trait B" and resolving to try again. She slips away to meet her fellow conspirators and finds the cache gone, the floor scratched where it stood, and Samonas waiting in the dark to talk candidly.

## 7 · Achilla / Kandawire

Achilla, a mercenary made obsolete by the peace the Emperor imposed, has watched a Custodian kick over a tank and tear it apart, and concluded the gold and the nobility are a sham. He marches to join a force gathering against the Imperium, not knowing or caring whose orders sent him.

Kandawire meets Valdor a second time and asks about a rumoured secret programme of enhanced leadership for the armies, kept from the Council. Valdor answers that there are no new generals for the Imperial Army — true, as far as it goes — and offers to tell her about the incident that started the rumour.
