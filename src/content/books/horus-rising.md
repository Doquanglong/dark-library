# Synopsis

It is the 31st millennium. Under the benevolent leadership of the Immortal Emperor, mankind has spread across the galaxy in a great crusade to reclaim the stars. Warmaster Horus, newly raised to command in the Emperor's absence, leads the 63rd Expedition — and finds the weight of the title heavier than any of them expected.

# Feelings

Slower than I expected, and better for it. Abnett spends the first third making you like these people so that the fall actually costs something. I know how this ends and I still don't want it to.

The Mournival scenes are the best part — four friends who genuinely like each other, and you can already see the cracks.
